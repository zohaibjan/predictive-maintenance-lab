From sensor reading to review

Local replay and Kafka share the same validation, causal feature engineering, scoring and storage code.

01

Ingest

Kafka producer or offline replay. Events keyed by asset.

02

Engineer

Six-reading rolling mean and trend. No future observations.

03

Score

Calibrated SVM or neural network. Threshold chosen on validation assets.

04

Review

SQLite persistence, risk dashboard and human prioritisation.

Kafka offsets are committed after durable ingestion or quarantine. Duplicate event IDs are idempotent; late events are rejected. Local range flags are basic diagnostics, not proof of model drift.
